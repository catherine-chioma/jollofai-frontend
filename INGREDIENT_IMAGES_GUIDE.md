## Image Specifications

- **Size**: 800x600px minimum (4:3 aspect ratio preferred)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Quality**: High quality, clear, well-lit
- **Style**: Clean, appetizing, authentic
- **Background**: Simple, preferably white or neutral

## Image Sources

- High-quality African ingredient photos
- Local markets or specialty stores
- Professional food photography
- Ensure proper licensing for commercial use

## Fallback Strategy

The UI includes error handling - if images fail to load, the ingredient cards will display without images but maintain full functionality.
